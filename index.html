FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What makes Scrollix different from other social media tools?

Three things. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription — pay once, own it forever. It runs locally on your computer, so your data stays with you. And the AI only optimizes — captions, hashtags, timing. You create 100% of your content.

Does Scrollix create content for me?

No — and that's deliberate. You create all of your photos, videos, and posts. Scrollix suggests captions (12 ideas in 4 tones), hashtags (15 per post), and posting times, and you review and approve everything before it's scheduled.

What do I need to get started?

Three things, and the built-in setup wizard walks you through each one:

  • An Instagram professional account (free to switch in Instagram's settings)
  • Your own Google Gemini API key (free tier available) — this powers the AI suggestions
  • Your own Google Cloud Storage bucket — media is staged there during publishing

Bringing your own key and bucket is what keeps Scrollix subscription-free and keeps your data under your control — nothing routes through our servers, because we don't have any.

Where is my data stored?

On your machine. Your posts, schedules, and account data live in a local database on your computer. Media files are staged in your own Google Cloud Storage bucket during publishing. Scrollix LLC operates no servers and holds no copy of your data.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes — a 14-day Money Back Guarantee: if within 14 days you experience an issue that makes Scrollix unusable and we're unable to resolve it, contact our support team for a 100% refund. It doesn't cover change of mind or features Scrollix doesn't claim to offer — see the full Refund Policy.

What's included in the purchase?

All features, unlimited Instagram accounts, activation on up to 2 computers, and free updates for version 1.x. There is nothing else to buy inside the app.

Which Instagram post types are supported?

Single-image posts, carousels, and Reels.

Is my Instagram connection safe?

Yes. Scrollix connects through Meta's official, approved Instagram API using secure OAuth sign-in — we never see or store your Instagram password, and your access tokens are encrypted on your machine.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or later, or macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later, with 500MB of disk space.

Do I need to keep paying monthly?

No. One-time purchase means you own Scrollix. No monthly fees, no recurring charges, nothing to cancel.
